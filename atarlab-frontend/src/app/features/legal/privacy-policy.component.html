Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

This policy explains what information AtarLab ("we", "us") collects when you use this site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email, phone, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) when you register.
  • Order information: shipping/billing addresses, order history, and payment status (we never store your card number or UPI PIN — payments are processed by Razorpay, and we only receive a transaction reference and status).
  • Usage data: pages viewed and searches made, used to power features like order tracking, wishlist, and product recommendations.
  • Cookies: a strictly-necessary session cookie that keeps you signed in. See our Cookie Policy for details — we don't currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders, and to contact you about them.
  • To maintain your account and let you sign in across sessions.
  • To send order-status notifications and, if you've subscribed, product updates.
  • To improve the catalog and recommendations shown to you.

How we share it

We share order and payment details with Razorpay (payment processing) and with our delivery partners (for the address and phone number needed to deliver your order). We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your rights

You can view and update your profile and addresses from your account at any time. To request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account, contact us at the address on our Contact page.

Data retention

We retain order records for as long as required for accounting, warranty, and legal purposes. Account information is retained until you request deletion, except where we are required to keep it longer by law.

This is template privacy-policy content for a demonstration project and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before using this in a real store, have it reviewed against the specific data you collect and the jurisdictions you operate in (e.g. India's DPDP Act, GDPR if you serve EU customers).
