Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Strictly necessary

We set exactly one cookie today: an httpOnly session cookie that keeps you signed in between visits. It can't be read by JavaScript (on this site or any other) and is only ever sent to our own servers. Without it, you'd be signed out on every page load. This cookie is exempt from consent under most cookie-law frameworks because the site can't function as a logged-in experience without it — it's not shown a choice in the consent banner for that reason.

Analytics & advertising

We don't currently use any analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes in the future, they'll only be set after you accept them in the cookie banner, and this page will be updated to list what's added and why.

Your choice

You can change your cookie preference at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer.
