Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Returns

Unopened items in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund. Because fragrance is a personal-use product, we're unable to accept returns of items that have been opened or used, except where the product is defective or not as described.

How to request a return

Go to your order history, open the order, and use the cancel/return option while it's within the eligible window. For a defective or incorrect item, contact us via the Contact page with your order number and a photo of the item.

Refunds

Once a return is received and inspected (or a cancellation is approved before dispatch), refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card and UPI refunds via Razorpay typically reflect within 5-7 business days depending on your bank; Cash on Delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled from your account for free as long as they haven't been shipped yet. Once an order is out for delivery, it can no longer be cancelled — you're welcome to refuse delivery or return it under the policy above instead.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or doesn't match what you ordered, we'll cover return shipping and send a replacement or full refund, whichever you prefer.
